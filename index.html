Tournament predictions you can take apart.

AKJB Analytics turns KenPom ratings and national rankings into NCAA Tournament projections. Every pick comes from a short list of rules and point values, so you can see why a team scores high, then change the rules and watch who moves.

HURLEY Rankings show who gets in

HURLEY blends five national rankings (AP, Coaches, NET, KenPom and WAB) into one score and projects each team's tournament seed. Every ranking is capped at 40.

The score starts at 100, subtracts the team's average rank, then subtracts half its WAB rank. Drag the sliders to try it. In the tool, click any team for its full breakdown, or open Bracketology for a projected 76-team field.

The Champion Model picks one winner

Every rule has a cutoff and a point value. A team that meets a rule earns the points, and penalty rules take points away. The highest score each season is the projected champion.

Switch rules on and off to see how a score builds. In the tool, change any cutoff or point value in the sidebar and every team re-scores instantly.

The Semifinal Model, and the record so far

It scores teams the same way, then ranks them inside each region, so every region gets one projected semifinalist. Pick a season to compare those picks with who actually reached the semifinals. Gold means the pick was right.

Champion Model picks by season

These use the default rules. A good fit to past seasons does not guarantee future results.

How a pick gets made

1Collect

Ratings and ranks come from KenPom, the AP and Coaches polls, NET and WAB.

2Score

Each team earns points for every rule it meets and loses points for every penalty.

3Rank

The highest score wins. Ties are settled by the tiebreakers listed under Methodology.

4Adjust

Change a cutoff or point value in the sidebar. Reset To Defaults puts everything back.
